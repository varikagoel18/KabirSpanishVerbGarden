§1 · Pico's 5 emotional states

The same character, five moods. Every visible Pico across the app is one of these. Simple SVG at v1; illustrated art at v2.

Curious
Default · head tilted · always present
Cheering
Correct answer · lesson complete
Thinking
While kid types or chooses
Comforting
Wrong answer · child struggles
Sleepy
After 8pm · yawn nudge at 15 min
Rule: only ONE emotion is on-screen at a time. Transitions between them are ~200ms fades — never abrupt. On iPhone this is paired with a soft haptic tap when Pico appears.

§3 · The kid's emotional arc

How the child feels over their first year with Pico. Each stage lasts weeks, not sessions. The line never dips into frustration.

Design implication: features must be sequenced to unlock in this order. A bond-level-5 personal moment shown on Day 1 would feel weird. The Kindness Missions engine can start firing at Discovery (Week 1) once the child has enough context. Steward-stage features (like "adopt an animal") aren't v1.
